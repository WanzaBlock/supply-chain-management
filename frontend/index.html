Process

How It Works

Every product batch is registered on-chain and tracked at every step of its journey.

01

Register Batch

Manufacturer registers a product batch on-chain with origin, expiry, and metadata stored immutably.

02

Transfer Custody

Each handoff between manufacturer, distributor, and retailer is recorded as an on-chain transfer event.

03

Regulator Review

Regulators access the full audit trail, flag suspect batches, and export compliance reports at any time.

04

Consumer Verify

Consumers scan the QR code to instantly verify product authenticity and view the full chain of custody.
